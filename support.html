Frequently Asked Questions

How do I start playing?
On the first screen, tap “Continue as Guest” to jump straight in — no account needed. You can set a username later to appear on the leaderboard.

What game modes are there?
Single Player (vs AI), Two Player (same device), and Online matchmaking against other players.

Is there real-money gambling?
No. All budget and currency in the game is virtual and has no cash value. There is no wagering and no cash-out.

I lost my progress / changed devices.
Your progress is saved to the cloud when you’re signed in. Make sure you use the same account. If something looks wrong, email us with your username.

How do I delete my account and data?
Email us at berkeedeemir@gmail.com from any address and include your username. We’ll remove your data within a reasonable period.
